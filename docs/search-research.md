## SEO, AEO and GEO implementation

Google states that conventional SEO applies to AI Overviews and AI Mode. It requires no special AI schema or new AI text file. Important content should be readable text, internally linked and crawlable; structured data must agree with what visitors can see. Eligibility does not guarantee indexing or AI inclusion. [Google: AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)

Bing recommends clear headings, tables and FAQs, evidence for claims, current information and consistent entities across text and imagery. Its AI Performance reporting measures citations, not rankings or authority. Accurate local business details also matter. [Microsoft Bing: AI Performance guidance](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/2026/2/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview/)

Implementation recommendations:

1. Render the page's essential content in HTML. Provide distinct titles/descriptions, one clear primary heading, semantic sections, breadcrumbs and descriptive links. Use extensionless canonical routes, a real XML sitemap, correct HTTP statuses and accessible robots directives.
2. Make each page useful for a different task: homepage overview, service detail, visual inspiration, calculator, measurement instruction, business identity or contact. Do not paste the same full service text and hero image onto every page.
3. Answer actual customer questions near the relevant service: supply only versus installation, what measurements/photos to send, areas covered, how a quotation is prepared. State the answer directly before additional detail.
4. Keep a single consistent business identity. Add LocalBusiness/appropriate subtype structured data containing verified name, phone, address and service area. Do not invent opening hours, coordinates, reviews, ratings or prices. Validate the markup after deployment. [Google: LocalBusiness structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business)
5. Add breadcrumb data matching navigation and article data only for a real guide with honest authorship/dates. FAQ text can help readers without promises of a rich search result. No unsupported “certified”, “best”, guaranteed lifespan or technical safety claims.
6. Label AI illustrations as concepts. Alt text should describe the actual image and application, not present generated images as completed SYAZE jobs. Keep claims about product composition and installation grounded in confirmed supplier documentation.
7. Keep calculator assumptions visible. A vertical-slat estimate is not a stock-board purchase quantity or a construction plan. Mention end gaps, separate spans, posts, gates, stock lengths and cutting losses as factors requiring confirmation. Avoid price estimates until actual rates are supplied.
8. Use responsive compressed images with dimensions, sensible loading priorities, large readable text, accessible controls and reduced-motion support. Readability and speed support the enquiry journey.
9. Before launch, set the actual public origin for canonical URLs/sitemap; validate live routes and structured data. Verify Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools using the owner's accounts, then monitor enquiries, crawl/index coverage and query performance. Business listings should reflect confirmed details; do not create duplicate offices.
